Rules that outrank preference
Text contrast meets 4.5:1 against its composited background, not against white.
Blur never sits under dense tables or over photographs.
Sensitive identifiers are masked by default; revealing one is an audited event.
Destructive or irreversible actions state who approves them before the button.
No screen invents a colour, a radius, or a font weight outside this sheet.
Hit targets on assisted-registration devices stay at or above 44px.
