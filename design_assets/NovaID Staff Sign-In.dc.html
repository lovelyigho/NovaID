NICRS Console

Staff and institutional access

One console for every NICRA role. Your permissions come from the account an administrator created for you — signing in never asks you to choose a role or a region.

There is no sign-up. Accounts are provisioned by an administrator above you and activated by you.
Multi-factor authentication is required for every privileged user, without exception.
Successful and failed sign-ins are both recorded, with device and location where permitted.
Accounts are never shared. Using another officer's credentials is a disciplinary and criminal matter.
Citizens do not sign in here. Citizen access is through Log in with NovaID on the public service.
